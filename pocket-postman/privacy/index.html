Privacy Policy for Pocket Postman

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pocket Postman ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Alphoria Systems ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

On first launch the app asks for your birth year. We only keep your age group (under 13, 13–15, or 16+) on your device to choose the right kind of ads. The birth year itself is not saved or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only where allowed; see below),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app,
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Children and teenagers: for players under 13, ads are child-directed: the advertising identifier is not used and no personalised ads are shown. Players aged 13–15 do not receive personalised ads. Ads for all players are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, UK and some US states you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission before your activity can be used for tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you on our servers. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress. To ask Google about data related to ads, use the links above.

Security

The app contains no web links that load code, and all game files are bundled inside the app. Data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed so that under-13 players receive only child-directed advertising, in line with COPPA and the Google Play Families policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
